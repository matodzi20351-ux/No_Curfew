UNCAST ERA is operated by the UNCAST ERA team in East London. Contact us on WhatsApp at 060 841 8390 with privacy questions or requests.

Shopping on this device

The bag and saved favourites use your browser’s local storage. These remain on this device until you remove them or clear site data. The guest checkout does not save your name or delivery area in local storage. Use your browser’s site-data settings to clear saved shopping choices.

WhatsApp requests

When you prepare a request, its text includes your name, selected items, options, delivery area, notes and request reference. Clicking the WhatsApp link passes this text to WhatsApp; pressing Send there shares it with us. WhatsApp and your bank process information under their own privacy terms. Avoid including identity documents or banking credentials in order notes.

How we use order information

We use information you send to answer questions, confirm availability, match payments and arrange delivery. Delivery information may be shared with the delivery service when needed to fulfil an agreed order. We keep relevant records for fulfilment, customer support and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; some records may need to be retained.

Hosting and optional accounts

Render hosts this website and may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details. If customer accounts are enabled, Supabase handles authentication and stored orders. Guest WhatsApp ordering does not require an account. Contact us about account records.

Advertising

Google ads and advertising analytics are not enabled in this release. Before enabling them, we will update this notice with the actual providers, cookies, purposes, available choices and required consent controls. We do not currently set advertising cookies through our own code.

Payment information

Payments happen inside your banking app. UNCAST ERA never needs your banking password, PIN or OTP. We use payment references and receipt information to match payments to confirmed orders.

Welcome coupon: this browser may store your WELCOME20 claim so it remains available on your next visit. Remove it in your bag. First-order eligibility is checked manually when you request an order.
