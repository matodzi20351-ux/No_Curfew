A laptop cover that actually fits

NO CURFEW editorial · 4 November 2026 · Practical guide

AI-generated concept illustration.

A screen size is not a model number

Two laptops with the same screen size can have different bodies, hinges and port positions. A fitted hard cover needs to match the chassis, not only the display measurement. Do not order a cover based on “14 inch” alone.

Find the exact model

Look for the product label on the underside or the model name in your computer’s system information. You can share the make and model with the seller without sharing a serial number, login details or other private information. If you send a label photo, cover identifiers that are not needed for a fit check.

Ask about the cutouts

Check access to charging, USB, audio and display ports. Ask for photographs of the underside and hinge area. A cover must not block cooling vents or prevent the lid from moving normally. Designs that look similar in a front-on picture can have very different clips underneath.

Fit it gently

Follow the supplier’s instructions and never force clips that do not line up. Stop if the cover pushes on the hinge, blocks a vent or makes the lid difficult to close. A fitted cover helps protect the outside surface, but it does not make a laptop drop-proof or water-resistant.

Choose a graphic after confirming fit

First establish compatibility, then choose the colour and artwork. Ask whether the item is a rigid cover or a decorative skin so you know what you are buying.
