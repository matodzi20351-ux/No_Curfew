Plan an LED room setup before buying

NO CURFEW editorial · 4 November 2026 · Practical guide

AI-generated concept illustration.

Measure the route, not just the wall

Use a tape measure or a piece of string to follow the exact route you want to light. Include corners and the distance to a suitable socket. A 5 m strip does not necessarily cover a whole room. Sketch where it starts and ends so you do not discover a shortfall after peeling off the adhesive backing.

Choose one lighting zone

A desk edge, a headboard or one shelf can create a strong effect without lighting every wall. Position the strip so you see its glow rather than looking directly at bright LEDs. Use separate practical task lighting for reading. Decorative coloured light is a mood choice, not a substitute for a comfortable study light.

Check permission and the surface

If you rent or live in residence, ask what fixing methods are allowed. Adhesive can lift paint or leave marks, especially on weak surfaces. Test an appropriate removable fixing in a hidden area and follow the product instructions. Do not cover ventilation openings or put the power supply under bedding.

Check the actual product

Ask whether the power adapter, controller and remote are included. Confirm the required power supply and whether the strip is suitable for the intended indoor location. Do not assume it is waterproof or that it can be cut at any point. Follow the marked cut points and installation instructions, and keep electrical parts away from water.
