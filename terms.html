Know before you order.

Updated 4 November 2026

Who we are

NO CURFEW is a student-led shop by the NO CURFEW team in East London. Contact: WhatsApp 060 841 8390. We confirm seller and fulfilment details directly before accepting payment.

Requests and prices

Submitting a WhatsApp message requests an order. It is not an automatic acceptance or stock reservation. T-shirts are R200, hoodies R350, fitted laptop covers R180 and 5 m LED strips R100. Installation is R20 per strip when selected. Other listed prices are estimates until confirmed. The final total, any PAXI fee and availability are agreed before payment.

Design images and compatibility

Product and lifestyle images are AI-generated concepts. Ask for actual product specifications and available product photographs before payment. Screen colours can vary. For laptop covers, send the exact make and model; screen size alone does not establish compatibility.

Payment and delivery

Pay only after we confirm the order details and recipient. We verify funds directly in the bank account. Delivery and pickup dates are agreed on WhatsApp. Read our payment and delivery guide.

Changes, cancellations and problems

Contact us with your reference as soon as you need to change or cancel a request. If an item is incorrect, damaged or defective, contact us with a description and relevant photographs so we can discuss the appropriate next step. Do not send an item back without arranging the return destination. We will explain applicable cancellation, return and refund arrangements before payment, including any conditions for custom work. Nothing here removes rights provided by applicable consumer law.

Confirm before committing

Ask for materials, measurements, final artwork, delivery charges and return arrangements before authorising payment. If anything is unclear, wait for our response. We do not promise stock, payment success or delivery based on the website alone.
